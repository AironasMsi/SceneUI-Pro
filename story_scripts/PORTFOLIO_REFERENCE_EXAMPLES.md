**Tekstas (Voiceover):**
> "You're born in Osaka, Japan. Your father was Chinese. A factory worker who disappeared when you were four. Left for a pack of cigarettes and never came back.  
> Your mother is Japanese. Works double shifts at a laundry service. Comes home smelling like detergent and exhaustion.  
> In Japan, blood matters. Yours is wrong. Half Chinese, impure.  
> The kids at school have a word for people like you. They use it often. Your teachers hear everything. They do nothing. You learn early that some people are born belonging. You were born outside, looking in through glass you can't break.  
> The beating happens when you're 15. Three older kids from a different school corner you in a park near the train station. They've seen you before. They don't like your face. They take turns. Fists, kicks, a wooden stick one of them brought from home. You curl up on the ground and wait.

> Because this isn't about finding a spot, it's about finding you. Behaviorists who study feline social patterns call this 'contact-seeking behavior.' Your cat isn't accidentally in your way. They've calculated your path, positioned themselves directly inside it, and waited.

## 🎯 Bendros visų skriptų taisyklės (Formulė)

1. **Nulis tuščiažodžiavimo (Zero Fluff):** Nėra žodžių kaip "delve", "tapestry", "in a world where...", "testament to...".
2. **Juslinės detalės (Sensory anchors):**
   - "Comes home smelling like detergent and exhaustion"
   - "Smells like the man who wore it before you and nobody has told you where he went"
   - "TV volume maxed out like he was trying to disappear into the noise"
   - "You can hear what's wrong with a motor the way a musician hears a flat note"
3. **Ritmas:** Trumpas sakinys. Ilgesnis sakinys. Vieno žodžio arba fragmentuotas sakinys. Pauzė.
4. **Emocinis atpažįstamumas:** Personažas visada kovoja su statusu, atmetimu, alkio jausmu ar noru būti pastebėtam ("Talks to you like you matter", "He looks at you. Not through you.").
5. **Pabaigos kabliukas (Retention cliffhanger):** Niekada neužbaigiama moralu – užbaigiama momentu, verčiančiu žiūrėti kitą dalį.
