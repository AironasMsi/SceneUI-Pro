Level 1: The programs. You're 16 and you make 20 pounds a match. You stand outside Turnstile 4 with a canvas bag of programs from 2 in the afternoon until kickoff. Two pounds each. You keep nothing from the sales. The 20 is a flat rate. Cash handed to you by a man named Pete, who has run match day staff here since before you were born. You do it for nothing. Everyone knows that, including Pete, which is probably why it's still 20.

The ground holds 7,200 and hasn't been full since 1998. On a decent Saturday, you get 3,800. The main stand is wooden. The away end is uncovered terracing, which means when it rains, 200 visiting fans stand in it and sing about it.

Your dad brought you here when you were 6. He has stood in the same spot in the Kop end for 31 years. He's standing there today. He'll be standing there when this story gets much worse.
